# INVEX 📦📊

## Smart Inventory, Billing & Business Analytics Management System

INVEX is a **full-stack inventory, billing, and business analytics management system** designed to simplify business operations through centralized product management, sales billing, stock tracking, and financial analysis.

The platform provides:

* Product and inventory management
* Multi-variant product handling
* Customer billing and sales management
* Real-time stock updates
* Profit and loss analytics
* Detailed sales reports
* Category and product performance analysis
* Data-driven business insights

# Features

## Product & Inventory Management

* Category-wise product organization with dynamic product listing
* Manage products with multiple variants, types, weights, pricing, and stock details
* Flexible MongoDB-based variant storage for different product structures
* Automatically updates stock levels after successful billing transactions
* Instant product search during billing operations
* Variant-level inventory tracking and management

---

## Billing & Sales Management

* Generate customer bills with multiple products and quantities
* Dynamic product selection through category, product, variant, and type hierarchy
* Automatically calculates item totals and final billing amounts
* Maintains complete customer billing records
* Stores product details, quantities, and prices for each transaction
* Records seller transaction details including cost price and selling price

---

## Profit & Business Analytics

* Provides detailed profit and loss analysis for business performance tracking
* Generates daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, and custom date-range reports
* Analyzes sales performance based on category, product, and product variants
* Calculates total cost, total sales, total profit, and quantity sold
* Provides average sales, cost, and profit analysis based on active business days
* Helps businesses understand overall financial performance

---

## Stock & Sales Insights

* Tracks current inventory and product stock availability
* Provides variant-level stock monitoring
* Identifies high-performing products
* Identifies profitable categories and products
* Provides insights into sales trends and inventory movement
* Supports data-driven business decision-making

# Architecture

INVEX follows a **full-stack architecture** consisting of a modern Next.js frontend, Django REST backend, and dual-database architecture.

### Frontend

Next.js and React.js provide the user interface for product management, billing operations, inventory tracking, and analytics dashboards.

### Backend

Django and Django REST Framework handle business logic, API operations, billing transactions, inventory updates, and analytical processing.

### Database

PostgreSQL manages structured business and transactional data, while MongoDB provides flexible storage for product variants and dynamic product structures.

### Data Processing

SQL queries and MongoDB aggregation pipelines are used to process sales, inventory, and business analytics data.
